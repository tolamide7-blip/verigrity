DNS Checker

Look up DNS records for any domain — A, AAAA, MX and NS. Useful for verifying mail setup, hosting and nameservers.

Records checked

  • A records (IPv4)
  • AAAA records (IPv6)
  • MX records (mail servers)
  • NS records (nameservers)

Limitations

This tool reports observable signals only. It is not a security audit, a business verification, or proof of fraud. Always verify independently.
