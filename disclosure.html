Disclosure

Last updated: 29 September 2026

This page explains how Verigrity funds its free tools and content, and how we keep commercial relationships separate from editorial decisions.

Advertising

Verigrity may display advertising, including Google AdSense, to help fund free tools and editorial content. Advertising is separate from our editorial explanations and tool results. The presence of an advertisement does not mean we endorse the advertised product or service, and it does not influence how our tools evaluate any website.

We do not ask users to click advertisements. We do not reward users for clicking them. Advertisements are served by third-party networks with their own policies and controls.

Affiliate links

Some Verigrity articles may contain affiliate links. If you click an affiliate link and make a qualifying purchase, Verigrity may receive compensation at no additional cost to you. Affiliate relationships will not change the way we describe the limitations of a product or service.

Where an article contains affiliate links, we will disclose this clearly near the top of the article, not only in this page.

Editorial independence

We aim to distinguish sponsored or commercial relationships from editorial content. Automated tools and reports should be treated as informational signals, not endorsements. Verigrity's editorial decisions — including what to cover, how to describe a tool's limitations, and which signals are included in a report — are made independently of any commercial arrangement.

Tool disclosure

Verigrity's free website checker is a preliminary signal tool. It does not independently certify businesses, domains, identities, documents, transactions, or security. A Verigrity Trust Score summarizes observable signals; it is not a security audit, a business registration check, or a fraud determination.

What we do not do

  • We do not accept payment in exchange for a favorable trust score.
  • We do not allow advertisers to influence tool results or editorial content.
  • We do not sell user data.
  • We do not accept payment to remove a website from a report.
  • We do not ask users to click advertisements.

Sources of funding

Verigrity is currently an independently operated project. It may be funded by advertising, affiliate relationships, or other clearly disclosed commercial arrangements. Any future funding sources will be disclosed on this page.

Corrections and complaints

If you believe a disclosure on this page is inaccurate, or if you have a complaint about advertising or affiliate content, please use our contact form and describe the issue.
