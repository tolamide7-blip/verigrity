Document Integrity

Inspect a file's cryptographic identity and available metadata. Any file type can be selected; deeper fields depend on the format and the information stored inside it.

Important: A hash proves that two copies have the same bytes; it does not prove who created a file, whether its contents are true, or whether a signature is genuine. Creation and modification dates can be missing, changed or generated by software. Verigrity does not claim to prove document authenticity from metadata alone.
