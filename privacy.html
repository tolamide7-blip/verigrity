Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Verigrity respects your privacy. This policy explains how information is handled when you use verigrity.com (the "Site") and our verification tools.

Information you provide

The Site does not require an account to use the basic checker. If you subscribe to our scam alerts mailing list, we collect the email address you provide. If you contact us through our contact form, we receive the information you choose to include in your message.

Website checker and tool queries

When you submit a website URL to the Verigrity Trust Checker or any Verigrity tool, the URL is sent to our server endpoint (/api/trust) so that the tool can fetch the site's public signals — HTTPS status, TLS certificate details, HTTP response headers, DNS records, and page content. Our server processes the URL in memory to produce the report. We do not store, log, or build a history of the URLs you submit. If the server cannot be reached, the checker falls back to a limited client-side assessment in your browser.

Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies for the following purposes:

  • Essential cookies. Necessary for the Site to function — for example, remembering your cookie consent choice.
  • Analytics cookies. Provided by Google Analytics 4 (via gtag.js) to help us understand how visitors use the Site.
  • Advertising cookies. If advertising is enabled, third-party vendors — including Google — use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this Site or other websites.

Non-essential cookies (analytics and advertising) are loaded only after you click "Accept" on our cookie banner. If you click "Reject non-essential," those cookies are not loaded.

Google AdSense and third-party advertising

We may use Google AdSense to display advertisements. When ads are displayed:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this Site and/or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our Site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You may also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.
  • Learn how Google uses data on partner sites at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we will request your consent before serving personalized advertising, in accordance with applicable law and Google's EU User Consent Policy.

How we use information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Site and its tools
  • To respond to messages you send us
  • To send scam alerts and updates if you subscribed
  • To display advertising (if enabled) and measure its performance
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues, abuse, or fraud

Third parties we work with

  • Google Analytics 4 — website analytics (privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy)
  • Google AdSense — advertising (if enabled)
  • Formspree — contact form processing (privacy policy: formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy)
  • Our hosting provider — for serving the Site and processing trust-check API requests

We do not sell your personal information.

Data retention

Email addresses collected for scam alerts are retained until you unsubscribe. Analytics data is retained according to Google Analytics' default retention settings. Tool queries are processed in memory and not retained on our servers.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us through the contact form linked below.

If you are in the EEA or UK, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Children's privacy

The Site is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

We use HTTPS across the Site and follow reasonable security practices. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

International transfers

Our service providers (including Google) may process data outside your country of residence, including in the United States. By using the Site, you consent to such transfers subject to applicable safeguards.

Third-party links

Our articles may link to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external sites.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent through our contact form. Submissions are processed by Formspree and forwarded to us.
