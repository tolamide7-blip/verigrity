How the Verigrity Trust Score works

Verigrity combines observable technical and transparency signals into a research aid, not a certification.

Signals

The service can examine HTTPS, HTTP status, redirects, TLS certificate information, DNS records, common security headers, page metadata and visible signals for privacy, terms, refund, contact and about information.

Limitations

  • Websites can block automated requests.
  • Content can change after a report is generated.
  • DNS and certificate data do not establish business ownership.
  • Technical configuration cannot establish the truth of every business claim.

Human verification

For important decisions, verify the organization independently, confirm payment details, review policies and investigate material claims through sources outside the website itself.
