Contact Verigrity

Send a message about the trust checker, our verification tools, privacy questions, corrections, or anything else. We read every message and reply when a response is needed.

What happens when you submit

Your message is sent securely through Formspree, a form-handling service, and delivered to our inbox. We do not sell or share your contact details. See our Privacy Policy for details on how form submissions are processed.

Response time

We usually respond within a few business days. If your message is about a specific tool or page, including the URL helps us help you faster.

Corrections and reports

If you've spotted an error in one of our guides, want to report a bug in a tool, or believe a Verigrity report about your site is inaccurate, please describe the issue clearly in the form above. Include any URLs, screenshots, or steps to reproduce if relevant.
