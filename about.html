ABOUT VERIGRITY

Independent research for a more verifiable web

Verigrity is an independent digital verification and research platform created by T.O., an independent remote researcher.

What Verigrity does

Verigrity provides free, no-signup tools that help people inspect websites, sources, claims, images, documents, emails and other digital evidence before relying on them.

  • Website trust and technical signals
  • Source and publisher information
  • Claim research and fact-checking workflows
  • Image metadata and available file evidence
  • Document metadata and file integrity
  • Email authentication headers
  • Domain, URL and security signals

Our evidence-first approach

Verigrity distinguishes between observable evidence, interpretation and conclusion. A signal can be useful without being conclusive.

Metadata can be changed or removed. An image can contain editing traces without proving who edited it. An email can pass authentication checks without proving that every statement in it is truthful. A document hash can establish file identity without establishing authorship.

For that reason, Verigrity avoids presenting automated checks as absolute proof when the available evidence cannot support that conclusion.

About T.O.

T.O. is an independent remote researcher and the creator of Verigrity.

Verigrity is independently operated. It is not presented as a government agency, law-enforcement organization, forensic laboratory, certification authority or official fact-checking body.

What Verigrity cannot guarantee

No automated online tool can reliably establish the complete truth or authenticity of every digital artifact or claim.

  • A website being completely safe or legitimate
  • An image being authentic or AI-generated
  • A document being genuine
  • A signature being legally valid or genuinely made by the claimed signer
  • An email being written by the apparent person
  • A claim being true or false solely from automated analysis
  • Metadata being complete or accurate

For formal authentication, forensic examination or legal determinations, users should consult the appropriate qualified professional or authoritative source.

Free access and monetization

Verigrity's core public tools are designed to remain free to use without a required account or paywall. The site may be supported by advertising, affiliate relationships or other clearly disclosed commercial arrangements.

Commercial support does not determine verification results. Advertisements, when displayed, are separate from editorial content and tool results. Verigrity does not ask users to click advertisements.

Privacy and transparency

Verigrity is designed to minimize unnecessary collection of personal information. Some tools can analyze files locally in the browser, while other tools may make server-side requests to inspect publicly accessible information.

See the Privacy Policy, Cookie Policy, Terms of Use and Disclosure for details applicable to the services enabled on the site.

Corrections and contact

If you identify an inaccurate statement, misleading result or broken tool, please use the Contact page and provide the page, specific issue and supporting evidence.
